# FRICTA Research Design

## Research Question

Which institutional factors most strongly predict digital adoption friction in Mexican childcare institutions?

## Main Hypothesis

Organizational constraints predict digital adoption friction more strongly than technical infrastructure constraints.

## Secondary Hypotheses

### H2

Higher administrative workload is associated with higher digital adoption friction.

### H3

Lower levels of process organization are associated with higher digital adoption friction.

### H4

Lower internet stability and lower computer access are associated with higher friction, but less strongly than organizational variables.

### H5

Higher perceived usefulness is associated with greater willingness to participate in pilot implementations.
