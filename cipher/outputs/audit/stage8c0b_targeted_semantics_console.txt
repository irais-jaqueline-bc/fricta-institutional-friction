0001: # Frozen Model-Selection Protocol
0002: 
0003: ## Candidate solutions
0004: 
0005: Evaluate K-Means, HAC-Ward, and diagonal-covariance GMM for k = 2, 3, 4, 5, 6
0006: on both R0 (standardized primary features) and R1 (PCA retaining at least 85%
0007: cumulative explained variance).
0008: 
0009: ## Exclusion rule
0010: 
0011: Discard any solution containing a cluster with fewer than 5 institutions.
0012: 
0013: ## Selection rule
0014: 
0015: 1. Compare internal validity using Silhouette, Davies-Bouldin, and
0016:    Calinski-Harabasz; use BIC additionally for GMM.
0017: 2. Retain competitive non-degenerate solutions.
0018: 3. Evaluate 1,000 80% subsamples without replacement.
0019: 4. Prefer the solution with the strongest median stability.
0020: 5. If solutions are practically tied, prefer:
0021:    a. higher Silhouette;
0022:    b. fewer clusters;
0023:    c. clearer institutional interpretation.
0024: 6. Do not change this rule after inspecting attractive visualizations.
0025:
